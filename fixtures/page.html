How to renew a permit

The permit holder shall renew the permit annually. Pursuant to section 4, renewals are processed by BLM in accordance with the schedule.

It is necessary to make a payment of the fee prior to renewal. A permit is not unlimited, and the agency will utilize your fee on a regular basis.

Send it by email and/or by mail. For questions, click here.

Except for farms, you will need to read the permit rules.
